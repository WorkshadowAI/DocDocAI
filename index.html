Frequently Asked Questions

Is the R35 Starter price per day or per week?

The R35 tier covers an entire week's full master lesson pack (including all daily lesson plans, hooks, teacher activities, and reinforcement exercises), NOT per day. For full terms (10–12 weeks), our R250 bundle gives you the entire term at a major discount.

Can I edit and reuse these documents next year?

Yes! All documents are delivered in fully editable Microsoft Word (.docx) and PDF formats with month/week-based headers. You pay once, easily adjust the dates next year, and keep the exact same curriculum materials permanently.

How does the "Second Version Free" guarantee work?

If your HOD requests specific formatting adjustments, or if you need an indigenous language dialect nuance updated (e.g., Siswati or isiZulu orthography), simply reply to our WhatsApp line. We will revise and reissue your file 100% free of charge.

We operate on an overnight delivery model. Send your ATP or topic on WhatsApp during the day or evening, and your ready-to-print master file will be delivered back to your WhatsApp before the morning bell.
